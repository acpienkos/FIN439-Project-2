## Video 1 · How the system and my thinking changed · 3:00 max

- **0:00–0:20.** “I used OpenAI Codex to help build, test, and document this app. The analysis and investment decision are mine. Education, not investment advice.” State this only after your own review.
- **0:20–1:10.** Explain a genuine change in your thinking. Show the prior Lab 13 mandate and the move from a raw qualifying mix to separating the mathematical result from operational trade approval. Attribute what drove your own change.
- **1:10–2:30.** Show Codex's proposed 90/10 result and the tool output. Independently reproduce the weighted return less the 20 bp drag, then compare direct weighted-monthly volatility with the covariance output. Explain what this check supports and what remains unverified.
- **2:30–2:55.** State your disposition in your own words and point to the validation record.

## Video 2 · Code and reproducibility · 5:00 max

- **0:00–0:20.** Use the disclosure and education statement above.
- **0:20–1:25.** Open the frozen repository and show setup, exact input placement and provenance. Start the app using the README.
- **1:25–3:15.** Walk through the CSV gate, dated BAA selection, annualized returns, weights, covariance, 1,771 grid entries and rule evaluation.
- **3:15–4:45.** Show the frozen base result and actual test output. Explain how to repeat a clean run and how unsupported inputs or unavailable data stop evaluation.

## Video 3 · Investment committee decision · 5:00 max

- **0:00–0:25.** Start with the app visibly running. State your disclosure, ownership, education statement and 0% live allocation ask.
- **0:25–1:45.** In the live app show the two baselines, benchmark, candidate, and open implementation gates. Explain the 4.20% modeled return and 1.66% volatility.
- **1:45–3:30.** Demonstrate an existing engineering stress after the locked experiment is complete. Explain the loss of modeled eligibility and how a fixed purchase date changes the policy.
- **3:30–4:45.** Defend the 0% decision and explain when you would revisit a 0–5% VT pilot. End by asking the committee to authorize evidence gathering and renewed review.
